Setup and tips

Capturing the iPhone screen

  • On a Mac, open iPhone Mirroring and start the School minigame.
  • Press Capture window and choose the iPhone Mirroring window. Chrome, Edge and Safari all support window capture.
  • Keep the game open. The board reshuffles if you minimize the game, which is why reading it from a mirror works well.

Using a screenshot instead

  • Press ⌃⇧⌘4, then Space, and click the mirroring window. That copies it to the clipboard.
  • Come back here and press ⌘V. The board is read straight away if the box is already set.
  • Live capture is blocked when this page is viewed inside Claude. The standalone file captures normally.

How it reads and solves

  • Each tile is matched by colour against red (−1), gold (0) and green (+1). Unsure tiles get a dashed outline.
  • When you fix a tile, it remembers that tile's look. Once it has seen all three values, it matches the whole tile, digits included.
  • The solver checks every combination of row and column taps and picks the highest score, then the fewest taps. Tap order does not matter.
  • Not every board can reach 14 or more. The score shown is the best this board allows.
